    Personality

    Mario is often shown to be impulsive, often doing things despite his peers telling him not to. Mario can be very kind and caring to his family when they're in danger.

    History

    Story of the Flowers

    Story of the Flowers is the first story Mario appears in. He doesn't have a major role outside of recieving a package with Peach.

    Relationships

    • Luigi: Luigi is Mario's brother and best friend. Though Mario often jokes in a bit of a mean way with Luigi, they both love each other very much. Mario is very protective of Luigi when danger arises.
    • Peach: Peach is one of the first people Mario met when first entering the Mushroom Kingdom. They'd become best friends and eventually start dating, later marrying. Peach is often the one adulting Mario and Daisy.
    • Daisy: Daisy is one of the members of the Core Four and Mario's later sister-in-law. They both often take jabs at each other, with Mario...being the one losing a lot of the time.

    Abilites

    As one of the seven Star Children, Mario possess extreme, superhuman abilites, such as:

    • Strength: Mario has been shown to be extremely strong, similar to other Star Children. He's been shown to be able to lift normally heavy objects with ease, and even picking up and kicking a small tower in Super Mario World.
    • Jump: Mario is able to jump about 5 times his own height, having one of the higher jump stats behind Luigi.
    • Durability / Resistance: All Star Children have a natural ability to resist most harsh elements. Mario is able to survive about the same conditions as the other human Star Children (Luigi and Peach). He's been shown to be able to temporarily survive lava. This ability also allows Mario to breathe in the low-oxygen enviroments of space and underwater.
    • Telekinesis: While Mario doesn't have Direct Telekinesis (the ability to move objects), both he and Luigi have Empathic Telekinesis. This is the bond between the brothers, allowing them to feel when the other is in danger and their condition.
    • Creation: Creation is the ability to generate elements on the fly. Mario's creation power is fire; he can use fireballs even without a Fire Flower, albeit less powerful than a Fire Flower. With a Fire Flower, Mario's creation abilites are amplified.

    Alongside the powers granted to the Star Children, Mario also has access to the various power-ups accross Aurelia.
