    Personality

    Mario is often shown to be impulsive, often doing things despite his peers telling him not to. Mario can be very kind and caring to his family when they're in danger.

    History

    Story of the Flowers

    Story of the Flowersis the first story Mario appears in. He doesn't have a major role outside of recieving a package with Peach.

    Relationships

    • Luigi: Luigi is Mario's brother and best friend. Though Mario often jokes in a bit of a mean way with Luigi, they both love each other very much. Mario is very protective of Luigi when danger arises.
    • Peach: Peach is one of the first people Mario met when first entering the Mushroom Kingdom. They'd become best friends and eventually start dating, later marrying. Peach is often the one adulting Mario and Daisy.
    • Daisy: Daisy is one of the members of the Core Four and Mario's later sister-in-law. They both often take jabs at each other, with Mario...being the one losing a lot of the time.
