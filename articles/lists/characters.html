Character Name

Contents

    A character is a person or other being who has a role in the story, be it major or minor. Characters are featured in every single story.

    Categories

    Characters are split up across several categories: Primary, Secondary, Notable, Minor, Mentioned, and Groups.

    • Primary: Primary characters are some of the most important characters to the series. These characters appear in nearly every story. This list rarely changes.
    • Secondary: Secondary characters are characters who are important to the series, but don't appear in most stories. They usually show up in a good portion of the stories, acting primarily as supporting characters.
    • Notable: Notable characters are characters that qualify for minor, but are important in the lore. This category usually includes rulers or other important characters in the world. This also includes recurring characters who would otherwise be one-offs.
    • Minor: Minor characters are mostly background characters that only have a couple of lines. They almost never appear again and mostly consist of generic members of a species, such as Toads or Yoshies.
    • Mentioned: Metioned characters are characters who are only mentioned by another character and do not have a speaking role. If they appear with a speaking or action role in a story, they do not qualify for this category.
    • Groups: Groups of characters are those who are minor characters that can be grouped by attributes.
