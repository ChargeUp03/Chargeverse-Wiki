Contents

    This page is a list of minor characters. Minor characters are typically generic members of a species (with exceptions) and appear in only one story, rarely in two. Minor characters usually have almost no role other than being background.

    List of Minor Characters (Pre-Modern Era)

    This section consists of minor characters from the pre-modern era, taking place from 2014-2018.

    Story of the Flowers

    Chief Yoshi (before modern Aurelia)

    After the Yoshi Village sees a comet crash on the nearby mountain, the Chief Yoshi at the time recruits a group of Yoshies to investigate the crash.

    Red Yoshi, Yellow Yoshi, Blue Yoshi, & Green Yoshi

    These Yoshies are recruited by the Chief to invesigate the comet crash, eventually bringing back the Fire Flowers and Ice Flowers.

    Luma 1 & Luma 2

    Luma 1 asks their colleague, Luma 2, if they should send the second comet; Luma 2 agrees.

    Mario & Luigi Discover the Mushroom Kingdom

    Spike

    Bullies Mario and Luigi at school. This is the Spike who also appears in the 2023 Super Mario Bros. Movie. Spike also appears in the short story Mario's Pre-Aurelia Arc, where he doesn't believe Mario has started dating Peach while Mario is still jumping between Earth and Aurelia.

    Wiggler

    After Mario sets off to find Luigi, he encounters a Wiggler. He accidentally steps on its leg, which causes it to wake up but fall asleep again. Mario looks back again to see it angry, chasing him down to the Kingdom Gate, where the Wiggler gets its head stuck in the gate.

    Kingdom Gate Guards

    The guards at the Kingdom Gate completely ignore Mario while they're preoccupied with the Wiggler getting stuck, buying Mario and Luigi more time before they found out they were unknowingly trespassing.

    Doctor's Office Receptionist

    Helps Mario and Luigi when they get to the office and directs them to the doctor.

    Doctor Toad

    Helps Luigi get a cast after being flung into a pillow factory. After that, they give Mario and Luigi an info card to a help center.

    Police Toad 1

    Finds Mario and Luigi walking through the kingdom and brings them to the police station without trying to cooperate with the bros. Later questions them about the Wiggler.

    Police Toad 2

    Police Toad 1's nicer partner who apologizes for having to put Mario and Luigi into custody temporarily. Later mentions Peach will come question them. After the questioning, they help the bros by giving them enough coins to rent a kart.

    Blue and Yellow Toad Guards

    These are NOT Blue and Yellow Toad. Both of them mess with Mario and Luigi when they try to enter the castle.

    Meeting Room Toad

    Leads Mario and Luigi to the castle's meeting room.

    Bank Toad

    Sets up Mario's account at the bank.

    Connections

    Math Teacher

    Teaches something related to math, to the Narrator's dismay.

    Luigi & Daisy's Night

    Waiter

    Comes up to Mario and Peach just for them to run out when they spot Luigi and Daisy leaving.

    Short Stories

    Destiny Del Vecchio

    In Mario's Pre-Aurelia Arc, Destiny rejects Spike, just to notice Mario trying not to laugh and promptly rejecting him too. She was also mentioned not by name in Crush

    Math Teacher (Earth)

    In The Disc, Mario's daydream gets interuppted when they ask him to answer a question.

    PE Teacher (Aurelia)

    In School, announces the class will be playing dodgeball and assigns team captains. Also asks Mario to bring Luigi to the health room when he gets hurt.

    History Teacher (Aurelia)

    In School, Daisy comments on how they always call the students formally by their last names. Later excuses Luigi after he returns from the health room.

    Store Clerk

    Gets the last Nintendo Switch for Daisy in Switch, to Mario's absolute dismay.

    List of Minor Characters (Modern Era)

    This section consists of minor characters from the modern era, taking place from 2019-2026.

    Racing Origins

    Baby Yoshi

    After Mario finds them at Star Road, they help Mario on his race across the island chain and helps find the way out of the Forrest of Illusion.

    Captain Toad's Grand Adventure

    The Bowser Revolution

    Mario, Luigi, and The Fountain of Time

    Boo

    Scares Luigi into the fountain, prompting Mario to jump in after him.

    The Kart's Revenge

    Message Koopa

    Alerts Bowser that their intellgence has concluded the karts have been hacked

    Ambush Kart 1, 2, & 3

    While Mario, Bowser, and Bowser Jr. fly to Sarasaland, a few karts ambush them. Mario attacks the first one, causing it to crash into the second one. The third one kidnaps Bowser Jr. by taking control of his Clown Car.

    Large Ambush Kart

    Appears after the smaller ambush karts are destroyed or fly away. Mario and Bowser attack it, but the kart manages to hit Mario. After he nearly falls into the ocean below, Daisy saves him with a Propeller Mushroom, and they both get into the kart's cockpit. While the kart resists Mario controlling it, Bowser rips out its logic board and enters the trunk of it. The trio uses it to get inside the parking tower the karts built.

    Dr. Mario's Return

    Toad Cook 1

    Serves Mario and Peach breakfast while also finding out him and Mario love the same exact, oddly specific breakfast combo.

    Toad Cook 2

    Serves Mario and Peach their breakfast alongside Toad Cook 2.

    Dr. Dolphin

    When Dr. Mario leaves with Mario and Luigi to reunite with the rest of the Jumpman family, Dr. Dolphin takes Dr. Mario's place temporarily.
