List of Mentioned and Implied Characters

Contents

    This page is a list of mentioned and implied characters. Mentioned and implied characters are when characters are mentioned by another character but do not appear in a story, or when another character is implied due to groups, naming of other characters, or another situations.

    List of Mentioned Characters

    This section consists of mentioned characters across all eras.

    Professional Wiggler Veterinarian

    Mentioned by Peach in Mario & Luigi Discover the Mushroom Kingdom when Mario brings up the Wiggler that got stuck in the kingdom gate while he was running from it.

    Traveler Toad (Captain Toad's Grandfather/Adventurer Toad's Father)

    When Captain Toad's father mentions how the headlamp has been passed down through generations in Captain Toad's Grand Adventure, he mentions his father, Traveler Toad, being part of the chain.

    Toadette's Parents

    After the group escapes from the climate island in Captain Toad's Grand Adventure, Toadette texts her parents when the fleet of airships appears on the horizon.

    Iggy Koopa

    When Bowser Jr. says his Switch 2 isn't working in The Bowser Revolution, he brings it to Bowser, who calls for Iggy to come and fix it. In The Kart's Revenge, Bowser mentioned Iggy had kitted his Clown Car out.

    Talking Flower Salesman

    While Daisy was giving Luigi directions in Shy Guy Bazaar in More Than Enough, she mentions that if he went too far, he'll run into a Talking Flower selling Phanto masks.

    Mario & Luigi's Uncles

    Mario mentions a game he and his uncles used to play in the short story Saying. They also gave Mario and Luigi a handful of N64 games in the short story N64 Games.

    Paper Bowser

    While Daisy wonders why she doesn't have a paper version in the short story Paper, she mentions Paper Bowser.

    Culux

    After Daisy asks Mario what his hardest battle ever was, he mentions Culux in the short story Boss Battle.

    Mozart

    Daisy calls Luigi 'Mozart' when commenting on his painting skills in the short story Creativity.

    Daisy's Therapist

    After using an alternate exclamatory phrase, Daisy mentions her therapist and the fact that Mario, Luigi, and Peach probably have one too in the short story Brightside.

    Mona

    Luigi and Daisy scroll through the WarioWare website's employee directory, while Daisy notes that Mona looks like her and Luigi. This combination exists in an alternate universe as heavily hinted at in the end of the story. Short story WarioWare.

    List of Implied Characters

    This section consists of implied characters across all eras.

    Brigade Yellow Toad

    While Brigade Yellow Toad doesn't have a speaking role in the story, he is implied to be present due to being part of the recurring Toad Brigade.

    Pauline I

    Pauline I is implied to exist due to Pauline II and Pauline III's naming.
